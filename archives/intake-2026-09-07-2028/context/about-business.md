# About the Business

A personal AI consulting practice run alongside a full-time role. It helps organizations move AI from experimentation and proofs of concept to reliable production systems.

## Offer

AI and GenAI strategy, AI platform architecture, enterprise AI integration, agentic AI solutions, MLOps/LLMOps, cloud-native AI platforms, and technical advisory. Positioning is about designing the underlying architecture, platforms, governance, integrations, and engineering practices that make AI genuinely useful and sustainable — not building chatbots.

## Ideal client

Small and medium-sized companies, startups, and enterprise teams that want to use AI but need help getting to production. Especially organizations that already have cloud, data, or software platforms and want to integrate AI into their products, internal workflows, or engineering processes.

## Revenue model

Pre-revenue. QuickBooks is the system of record for invoicing and tracking invoiced vs. paid. Engagement model to be defined as part of the Q4 offer one-pager (assessments, workshops, advisory).
